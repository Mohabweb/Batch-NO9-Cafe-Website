# Batch NO.9 — Website Build Plan

**Brand:** Batch NO.9 · Deli • Coffee • Dessert
**Location:** Stanley Branch, Alexandria, Egypt *(the menu PDF spells it "Stanely". Confirm the correct spelling.)*
**Goal:** A fast, mobile-first, minimalist website with a glassmorphism look that shows the menu clearly and gets people to visit, call or follow the cafe.

---

## 0. Brief for the building agent (read first)

Build a **minimal, glassmorphic, mobile-first** website for a specialty coffee and deli cafe.
- Colors come from the logo: **brick red + warm cream** (tokens in §2).
- The layout idea comes from the Luckin Coffee template: one large rounded "stage" card, a bold product hero, a vertical outlined and solid drink name, and carousel arrows (§3).
- Navigation must be obvious in one glance: 4 pages, a floating glass nav, and a persistent "Menu" action.
- Most visitors will arrive from Instagram or TikTok on a phone, so design for **390px wide first**, then scale up.
- Real assets are in the uploads: logo, menu PDF, brownie and cappuccino photo, social links, phone number.
- More photos of the place will be provided later. Use clearly marked placeholders until then.

## 3. Design language

### 3.1 What to take from the Luckin template
1. **A big rounded stage card** sitting on a solid background, with the whole hero inside it.
2. **Compact top-left nav** inside the card: Home · Menu · Beans · Visit.
3. **Left column:** a small label, a large two-weight headline, and two pill CTAs (one filled, one light).
4. **Right column:** a large product photo on a darker panel, with **vertical text** of the drink name (one solid, one outline) and **← →** arrows to cycle featured items.
5. High contrast and one hero product at a time.

### 3.2 Batch NO.9 adaptation
- Page background: `--brick`. Stage card: a darker brick/espresso tint or glass.
- Hero headline example: **"Every batch,** *made with care."* (Final copy is in §6.)
- Vertical text cycles through signature drinks (e.g. **CLOUD 9** / *LATTE*) next to a cutout or photo.
- The CTA pair becomes **"View Menu"** (filled cream) and **"Find Us"** (glass).

### 3.3 Glassmorphism rules (keep it tasteful)
Use glass only on **floating UI** (nav, menu cards, info panels, chips). Never on large body text blocks.

```css
.glass {
  background: rgba(232, 221, 201, 0.12);       /* cream tint */
  backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(140%);
  -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(140%);
  border: 1px solid rgba(232, 221, 201, 0.28);
  border-radius: 24px;
  box-shadow: 0 10px 40px rgba(60, 10, 5, 0.25);
}
.glass--dark { background: rgba(43, 26, 20, 0.35); }
```

- Glass needs something colorful behind it. Place blurred blobs (terracotta and a lighter red) and the photos behind the panels.
- Always provide a **fallback** for browsers without `backdrop-filter`: a solid `rgba(152, 47, 32, 0.85)`.
- **Contrast:** text on glass must be at least WCAG AA (4.5:1). Use cream text on the red glass. Use espresso text only on the lighter cream glass.
- Maximum 2–3 glass layers visible at once, so mobile stays smooth.

### 3.4 Minimalism rules
- One idea per screen, one primary action per section.
- No more than 2 fonts, 2 main colors, and 1 shadow style.
- Remove everything decorative that does not support food, drink or navigation.

## 4. Sitemap and navigation

```
Home (/)
Menu (/menu)
Beans (/beans)        ← "Coffee Beans: Map & Tasting Notes"
Visit (/visit)        ← location, hours, contact, socials
```

### Navigation behavior
- **Desktop:** a floating glass pill at the top center (logo left, 4 links, "Menu" highlighted). It stays fixed while scrolling and shrinks slightly.
- **Mobile:** logo top-left and a menu button top-right opening a full-screen glass overlay with 4 large links. Also add a **bottom glass tab bar** with: Home · Menu · Beans · Visit. Thumbs reach it easily.
- The active page is shown with a cream underline or a filled pill.
- Footer on every page: logo, address, social icons, phone, "Prices exclude tax".
- Maximum 2 taps from anywhere to the full menu, the address, or the phone dialer.

## 9. Motion
- Subtle only: fade and rise on scroll (200–400ms), hover lift on cards, slow float on background blobs.
- Hero slider transitions: crossfade plus slide of the vertical text.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: turn animations off.
- No autoplay video, no heavy parallax.

---

## 10. Technical recommendations
- **Stack:** Astro or Next.js, with Tailwind CSS. Static output is enough. Menu data lives in a JSON/TS file.
- **Mobile-first**, breakpoints at 390 / 768 / 1280.
- **Performance:** serve images as WebP or AVIF, lazy-load below the fold, keep the Lighthouse mobile score at 90 or higher, and use font-display swap.
- **Accessibility:** semantic HTML, visible focus states, alt text on all images, tap targets of 44px or more, AA contrast.
- **SEO:** page titles like "Batch NO.9 | Coffee, Deli & Dessert in Stanley, Alexandria". Add meta descriptions, Open Graph image (logo or the duo photo), and `Restaurant` / `CafeOrCoffeeShop` JSON-LD with address, phone and menu link.
- **Analytics:** a simple privacy-friendly option (for example Plausible or Google Analytics) with tracked events on "Call", "Directions" and "Menu PDF".
- **Hosting:** Vercel, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages, with a custom domain later.
